# Instagram Strategy — Vedic Astro

Instagram playbook to drive leads to the free Kundli form (`/kundli`), which feeds the `/admin` leads list.

---

## 1. Account Setup

- **Handle:** `@vedicastro` (or `@vedicastro.in`)
- **Profile pic:** Sun/mandala icon (clean, gold on dark — matches site)
- **Bio:**
  ```
  ✨ Authentic Vedic Astrology
  🪔 Real planetary calculations
  🔢 Life Path & Destiny Numbers
  Free Kundli 👇 link in bio
  ```
- **Link in bio:** Site → `/kundli` page directly

| Pillar | Format | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily Horoscope (moon sign) | Story + Reel | Daily story, 3x reel/wk | Reach & habit |

## 3. 7-Day Content Cadence

- **Mon:** Reel — "Your Life Path Number" (top 3 numbers, teaser → full tool on site)
- **Tue:** Carousel — "How to read your Kundli in 60 seconds"
- **Wed:** Reel — Daily horoscope for your moon sign
- **Thu:** Carousel — "5 Planets that control your luck" (use blog posts as source)
- **Fri:** Story poll — "Guess your element ☀️🔥🌊🌬️" + Reel teaser
- **Sat:** Live — "Free 5-min chart readings" (great DM magnet)
- **Sun:** Reel — Client testimonial + weekly numerology forecast

## 4. Lead Funnel

1. **Every post CTA:** "Get your FREE Kundli → link in bio"
2. **Story sticker "Kundli"** with link → `/kundli`
3. **DM automation:** Auto-reply for "kundli" → "Send your birth details & I'll prepare your free reading" (drives email capture)
4. **Live sessions:** End with "DM me your DOB" → convert to email
5. **Comment bait:** "Comment ☀️ and I'll DM your sun sign reading" → start DM conversations

## 6. Growth Tactics

- **30-60 min/day:** comment on big astrology accounts + reply to every comment on your posts (algorithm boost)
- **Collab 1x/week:** Reels with wellness/yoga/meditation creators
- **Giveaway monthly:** "Free premium reading" → entry = follow + DM birth date (huge lead source)
- **Repurpose 10 blog posts** into carousels — zero new writing needed
- **Consistency > volume:** 4-5 posts/wk beats daily burnout

## 7. Track (check weekly)

- **Saves** (education content) — carousels
- **Shares** (relatable content) — horoscopes, numerology
- **Profile visits + link clicks** → these become leads
- **DM inquiries** → email captures in admin
