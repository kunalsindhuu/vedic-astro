Vedic Astrology Explained

Vedic astrology, or Jyotish, is an ancient Indian system of astrology that dates back over 5,000 years. Unlike Western astrology which uses the tropical zodiac, Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac which accounts for the precession of equinoxes.


Key components of Vedic astrology include:

  • Rashi (Moon Sign): Your core emotional nature
  • Lagna (Ascendant): Your outer personality and life path
  • Nakshatras: 27 lunar mansions that add depth to your chart
  • Dasha System: Planetary periods that time major life events
  • Yogas: Special planetary combinations that indicate destiny
