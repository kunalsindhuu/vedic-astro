About Numerology

Numerology is the ancient study of the hidden meaning of numbers. In Vedic tradition, numbers are associated with the nine planets (Navagraha), and each number carries its own energy, strengths, and life lessons.


  • Life Path Number: Your birth date reduced to a single digit — your life's purpose
  • Destiny Number: Your birth name reduced — your talents and opportunities
  • Master Numbers (11, 22, 33): Powerful numbers that are not reduced, bringing exceptional gifts and challenges
  • Lucky Numbers & Days: Based on the ruling planet of your Life Path number
